# Code of Conduct

One maintainer, a small project, no committee. What follows is short
because it can be enforced as written.

## The standard

Take part as you would in a workshop with people you respect and do not
know well. Disagree about the code, the models and the data as sharply as
the subject deserves; keep it about the work.

Not welcome, and enough on their own to end participation: harassment,
insults, slurs, sexualised language or imagery, sustained hostility,
publishing anyone's private information, or persisting after being asked
to stop.

Two that this project runs into more than most:

- **Health data is personal.** Issues and pull requests may carry sleep,
  heart rate, weight or training history. Do not comment on anyone's
  numbers beyond what the technical question needs, and do not post
  another person's data at all. Generated data from
  `fetcher/seed_demo.py` reproduces almost every display bug.
- **Nobody here is your doctor, and nobody wants to be.** Do not give
  medical advice, and do not press others for it. This applies to the
  maintainer as much as to anyone else.

## Scope

Issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and code review in
this repository, plus anywhere someone is representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report privately to the maintainer, [@Imgrund](https://github.com/Imgrund),
using the contact details on that profile. Reports are read by one person
and stay with him.

The responses available are the ones GitHub gives a maintainer: a warning,
having a comment edited or removed, having an issue or pull request
closed, and blocking an account from the repository. Which one follows
depends on severity, and the choice is the maintainer's. Bad-faith
reports are themselves a violation.

Being right about the code does not exempt anyone from this, and being
wrong about the code is not a violation of it.

This is a personal project with no organisation behind it and no appeal
process. If that is not enough process for your situation, GitHub's own
[reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github)
reach people with more authority than a repository owner has.

## Attribution
